Educational short videos about the practical, spiritual, emotional, and thoughtful value of the Prophetic Sunnah.

About the service

Athar Tayib uses a private, self-hosted workflow to prepare short educational videos. A video is sent to the owner for review and is published to connected social accounts only after the owner gives explicit approval.

The workflow is operated by the account owner and is not offered as a public publishing service.
