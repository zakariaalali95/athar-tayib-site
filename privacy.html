Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

What this app does

Athar Tayib is a private, self-hosted content workflow used by its owner to prepare and publish educational short videos to the owner's connected social accounts.

Information processed

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the app may process the account's Open ID, display name, avatar, OAuth access and refresh tokens, and videos and captions selected for publishing.

How information is used

The information is used only to confirm the connected account, upload owner-approved content, check publishing status, and maintain the connection requested by the owner.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's local self-hosted n8n instance using encrypted credentials. Information is not sold, rented, or used for advertising. Data is shared with TikTok only as needed to perform actions explicitly approved by the owner.

Retention and deletion

The owner may disconnect TikTok access at any time. Local credentials and generated media can then be removed from the self-hosted workflow. TikTok access may also be revoked from the owner's TikTok account settings.

Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, contact the project through @asar.tayb.
